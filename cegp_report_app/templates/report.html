Methodology

Figures are drawn from Cloud Email Gateway Protection detection logs in Trend Vision One for the stated period, using Trend Micro's internal TMES sheet Potential Loss methodology: potential loss avoided = (detections in a TMES sub-type) × (IC3 loss-per-victim for that sub-type's category) × (days in the reporting period ÷ 91.25), summed by sub-type above. Unlike the earlier version of this report, this applies the full loss-per-victim figure to every detection with no discount for the fraction that would have plausibly succeeded — it mirrors Trend Micro's own internal reporting template rather than a probability-weighted estimate. Source for loss-per-victim figures: {{ ic3_source }}. Six TMES sub-types (Email Reputation Service, SPF Violation, Graymail, Scan Exception, Content Filtering, Attachment) have no IC3 category mapping in the source template and are shown as count-only. This is a directional figure for illustrating security value, not an audited financial-loss calculation. For a customer-specific, FAIR-model-based risk figure, use Vision One's Cyber Risk Quantification (CRQ) capability instead.
